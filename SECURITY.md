# Security Policy

8472backup is in early development and does not yet have a stable backup release. Until a supported-release policy is published, security fixes are handled on a best-effort basis by the project owner.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities in public issues or discussions. Report them privately by email to **mail@thomas-kuhlmann.de**.

Include, where possible:

- the affected commit, version, or component;
- steps to reproduce and the expected and actual behavior;
- relevant logs or proof-of-concept details, with secrets and backup contents removed;
- the potential impact and any known workaround.

Do not send real backup data, credentials, private keys, or unredacted secrets. The project owner will coordinate investigation and disclosure with the reporter. No response-time or remediation-time guarantee is currently established.

## Disclosure and fixes

Do not publish vulnerability details before a coordinated disclosure or public fix. Confirmed vulnerabilities will be fixed and documented in the changelog when appropriate. A CVE may be requested when the issue and distribution warrant one. Reporter credit is given if requested and safe to do so.

Security reports are handled independently of commercial support arrangements. The project does not offer automatic troubleshooting solutions for problems caused by use outside its documented operating rules.
